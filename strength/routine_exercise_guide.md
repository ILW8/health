# Strength routine — exercise guide

**Prepared:** 8 September 2026
**Plan reference:** [cycling/epic49_training_plan.md](../cycling/epic49_training_plan.md), "Strength (2× per week, 25–30 min)"

Beginner-level technique notes for the six exercises in the routine. Tue and Fri, 25–30 min. Restarted 8 Sep 2026 after a break since mid-June; see [notes/lower_back_episodes.md](../notes/lower_back_episodes.md) for the back history that shapes the RDL and plank cues.

## Structure

Three pairs, done as alternating sets: A then B, rest 60 s, repeat until three rounds of each, then move to the next pair.

| Pair | A | B |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Goblet squat 3 × 12 | Romanian deadlift 3 × 12 |
| 2 | Single-arm row 3 × 12 per side | Split squat or step-up 3 × 10 per leg |
| 3 | Push-ups 3 × 10–12 | Plank 3 × 35–45 s |

Warm-up first (5 min): easy movement, cat-cow × 8, bodyweight squat × 10, glute bridge × 10.

Progress by adding reps first, then weight. Keep 1–2 reps in reserve on every set. Friday sessions before a 100 km+ Saturday stay light. No strength in the 4 days before EPIC49.

## Exercises

### Goblet squat
Hold one dumbbell vertically by the top end (or a kettlebell by the horns) against the chest, elbows tucked under it. Feet shoulder-width, toes turned out slightly. Push the hips back and bend the knees together, sitting between the heels. Weight over mid-foot, chest up; go as low as the back stays flat (thighs parallel is plenty). Drive up through the whole foot. In on the way down, out on the way up. Knees track over toes, not inward.

### Romanian deadlift (RDL)
Dumbbell in each hand resting on the thighs, feet hip-width, knees slightly bent and fixed. Push the hips straight back so the weights slide down the front of the legs; torso tips forward with a flat back. Stop at the hamstring stretch, usually mid-shin. Never round the lower back to go deeper. Squeeze glutes to stand, hips coming forward. Weights brush the legs throughout.
**Back note:** go light. If the lower back rather than the hamstrings feels the work, shorten the range or drop to 3 × 8. Log anything that reproduces the on-bike pain in the back episodes file.

### Single-arm row
Left hand and left knee on a bench (or hand on a chair, feet staggered), back flat and roughly parallel to the floor. Dumbbell in the right hand, arm hanging straight. Pull the elbow up and back past the ribs, close to the body, until the dumbbell reaches the hip. Pause, lower slowly. No torso twist. Swap sides.

### Split squat (or step-up)
Long stride stance: one foot forward, the other back on the ball of the foot. Lower straight down, bending both knees, until the back knee is a couple of inches off the floor and the front thigh is about parallel. Front knee over the ankle, torso upright. Push up through the front heel. Hold a wall or chair for balance if needed. All reps on one leg, then swap.
Step-up alternative: step onto a knee-height box with one foot, drive up to standing tall, step down slowly.

### Push-ups
Hands slightly wider than shoulders, body in one straight line from head to heels, glutes and abs braced. Lower until the chest is a fist's width from the floor, elbows going back at ~45° rather than flaring sideways. Press back up. If 10 clean reps aren't there, put the hands on a bench or step.

### Plank
Forearms on the floor, elbows under shoulders, toes tucked. Straight line head to heels: squeeze glutes, tuck the ribs down slightly so the lower back doesn't sag. Breathe normally. When the hips drop or the lower back starts to feel it, stop — that's the set. Side planks or dead bugs are the substitute if the back is at all unsettled.
